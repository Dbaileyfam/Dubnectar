Band bio

Blending smooth reggae rhythms with infectious funk grooves, Dub Nectar creates an uplifting musical experience that gets everyone moving. Their vibrant performances combine island vibes with soulful funk elements, balancing deep-pocket rhythm sections with melodic hooks built for dance floors and festival crowds.

Quick facts

  • Genre: Reggae / Funk / Dub
  • Based in Utah
  • Performance style: High-energy live band set
  • Best for: Clubs, festivals, private events
